# Measurement & Review Framework

**Status: proposed measurement plan for proposed advisory programs. No commercial, operational, compliance, or ROI results are claimed.**

Edition: **15 November 2026**. No analytics code, tracking pixel, integration, test script, or monitoring deployment is included.

## Measurement principles

- Separate documentation publication, audience interest, approved delivery, and technical outcomes.
- Use approved existing analytics, authorized reports, or manual owner-maintained records. Missing telemetry is reported as unavailable, not zero.
- Establish a baseline and an owner before setting commercial outcome targets. Numbers below are documentary quality targets only where explicitly labeled.
- Report scope, period, numerator, denominator, exclusions, evidence source, and uncertainty. Do not imply causal impact from a small or uncontrolled sample.
- Never store personal data, client evidence, or sensitive supplier/agent details in this public repository. Public reporting should be aggregated or synthetic.

## Commercial interest: use only available, approved data

| Measure | Definition and caveat |
| --- | --- |
| Proposal-document engagement | Existing authorized page/repository analytics if available. Repository clones or views are not unique qualified buyers. If unavailable, report unavailable. |
| Cross-promotion referral visits | Visits attributed by approved existing analytics to a specific approved source placement. No placement is assumed live merely because it appears in the map. |
| Discovery starts and completions | Existing funnel events, if defined and accessible. Do not invent events or modify the form. Define the denominator and distinguish visits from completed requests. |
| Qualified Discovery inquiries | Requests meeting an owner-agreed program-fit definition, reviewed in approved private systems. Keep personal details out of public reporting. |

Suggested qualification fields for private operations are buyer role, trigger, in-scope workflow/supplier/model family, acknowledged exclusions, and an appropriate decision owner. Consent, access, and retention follow the existing approved business process; this project creates no new intake system.

## Program-specific evidence completion

### Agent Authority & Accountability Assurance

- In-scope action classes with named accountable owners and explicit authority decisions.
- Consequential actions with limits, approval conditions, expiry/reconfirmation expectations, and revocation owners documented.
- Unresolved enforcement-evidence gaps, assigned owners, and decisions to proceed or pause.
- Acceptance of the documentary mandate/tabletop record by the designated sponsor.

These measures assess documentation completeness, not prevented incidents, actual permission enforcement, or agent safety.

### AI Supplier Exit Rehearsal & Continuity Planning

- In-scope dependencies with an owner and source-backed exportability/rights status, including unknowns.
- Continuity and communication decisions with accountable owners.
- Tabletop blockers with evidence requirements, owner, and decision date.
- Acceptance of the rehearsal decision record and separately defined technical validation needs.

Do not report a completed tabletop as a successful technical migration, achieved portability, downtime reduction, or verified recovery time.

### AI Data Revocation & Model Retirement Assurance

- Known in-scope artifacts with owner, trigger relationship, disposition decision or unresolved state, and evidence reference.
- Supplier evidence requests with documented scope and response limitations, without assuming they have been sent or fulfilled.
- Distinct status for requested, supplier-reported, technically evidenced, and independently reviewed actions.
- Qualified privacy/model-risk acceptance of remaining limitations and retirement/restriction decisions.

Do not call a complete register proof of erasure, lawful processing, unlearning, or technical retirement.

After the commit, use live read-only HTTPS requests and report the actual method, observation time, response status, final URL/redirects, and TLS verification information available from the checking service. Zion's production repository must receive no write actions. Mark unavailable data explicitly. Do not create automatic scheduled reporting, send the report externally, or publish sensitive client evidence under this documentation scope.
