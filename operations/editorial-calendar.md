## Per-program validation questions

### Agent authority

Who owns agent-caused business outcomes? Which actions require exact-action approval? Where does technical access exceed business authority? Does an existing IAM/governance engagement already produce the proposed decision-rights record? Who would implement and evidence remediation?

### Supplier exit

Which real renewal or provider change motivates the work? What can the customer export, under what right, and with what evidence? Who owns continuity decisions? Is the proposed tabletop additional to the existing lock-in score or already part of another service?

### Revocation and retirement

What actual trigger and dataset/model family are in scope? Which derived artifacts are known versus inferred? Who makes legal-hold and rights decisions? What can suppliers attest to? What technical and legal limitations must remain unresolved rather than promised away?

Preparing these questions does not authorize sending emails, recruiting participants, or processing client evidence.

## Review cadence and stopping rules

- Review availability, CTA destinations, program status, and material claims immediately before every intended placement.
- Proposed operational target: review active source/claim records at least every 30 days, or sooner after an incident, catalog change, supplier update, or legal-guidance change. This is a target, not an achieved SLA.
- Maintain a weekly editorial decision log while validation is active and a monthly portfolio review thereafter if an owner accepts it.
- If evidence contradicts copy, pause the copy. If the proposal duplicates a current service, merge or stop it. If no delivery owner or necessary specialist reviewer is available, do not announce availability.
- Record decisions in the existing documents and [changelog](../CHANGELOG.md). Do not add code, automate posting, or change the production repository.
