# Zion Portfolio Operations Hub

**Status: published planning documentation for proposed advisory programs. These programs are not launched or bookable offers.**

Research snapshot and initial documentation edition: **15 November 2026**.

This public, Markdown-only repository contains portfolio research, proposed service positioning, draft promotional copy, and content operations guidance for the AI and IT ecosystem at [Zion Tech Group](https://ziontechgroup.com/). It is maintained under [saipradikmallakuntla-tech](https://github.com/saipradikmallakuntla-tech). It is not Zion's production website, an official availability catalog, a certification, or evidence that Zion has accepted delivery responsibility for the proposed programs.

## Executive recommendation

Develop three narrowly bounded, documentation-led advisory programs that build on existing Zion listings instead of adding another generic app:

| Proposed program | Buyer and decision | Proposed pilot boundary | Distinct output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Agent Authority & Accountability Assurance](solutions/agent-authority-assurance.md) | CISO, COO, automation owner: who may authorize an agent to act? | One write-capable agent workflow | Accountable owner, action-authority matrix, approval and revocation procedures, tabletop decision log |
| [AI Supplier Exit Rehearsal & Continuity Planning](solutions/ai-supplier-exit-rehearsal.md) | CIO, procurement, architecture: can we organize an orderly supplier exit? | One critical AI supplier | Export-rights and dependency register, continuity decisions, facilitated tabletop, blocker and acceptance records |
| [AI Data Revocation & Model Retirement Assurance](solutions/ai-data-revocation-retirement.md) | DPO, data governance, model-risk owner: what must change when AI data use or a model ends? | One dataset/model family | Derived-artifact impact map, retention and retirement decisions, evidence checklist, unresolved limitations register |

These are **proposed productized services, not software products**. No identical end-to-end program surfaced in the reviewed listings, but terse descriptions, stale material, and inconsistent catalogs prevent a categorical claim of uniqueness. Confirmation by the relevant Zion delivery owner is a mandatory gate before service promotion.

## Research basis and limitations

The read-only review covered the homepage, service and app hubs, selected adjacent product pages, public documentation in [Zion-support/zion-support.github.io](https://github.com/Zion-support/zion-support.github.io), the legacy service index containing 1,614 records, and all five linked flagship registry parts containing 421 unique app IDs. Those counts describe different inventories and dates; neither is a verified total of working or purchasable products.

Important findings include conflicting homepage/app-hub counts, a promoted governance destination displaying Coming soon, and historical catalog links returning not found. Existing listings already include quantum-safe migration, IAM, disaster recovery, synthetic-media detection, retention management, and vendor lock-in scoring; these should not be repackaged as wholly new areas. See the [catalog and gap audit](research/catalog-and-gap-audit.md) and [source register](research/source-register.md).

## What is and is not approved

The repository owner approved public publication of these 12 documentation files. That approval does not establish service availability, validate every source claim, assign a Zion delivery team, approve new prices, or authorize changes to Zion's website or other repositories.

All proposed homepage placements, sibling-app backlinks, outreach, translations, and commercial launch announcements remain subject to separate approval. ## Non-coding and data boundaries

- Only Markdown documentation belongs in this approved publication scope.
- No application code, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, TypeScript, scripts, tests, build files, workflows, analytics code, or deployment assets are included.
- Do not modify, fork for editing, open pull requests against, or publish changes to Zion-support/zion-support.github.io as part of this scope.
- Do not place client evidence, personal data, credentials, contracts, access tokens, or confidential infrastructure details in this public repository. Use blank templates or clearly labeled synthetic examples.
- Advisory documents do not enforce permissions, execute migrations, delete data, prove machine unlearning, or replace qualified legal and technical review.
- Source material remains attributable to its original owners. Publication of original planning text here does not grant rights over Zion's software, brand, or third-party documentation.
