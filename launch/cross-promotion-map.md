**Status: proposed editorial referrals for unlaunched advisory programs. This is a recommendation map, not a record of installed links or approved advertising.**

Research snapshot and first edition: **15 November 2026**.

## Operating principle

Use existing Zion tools and listings as context, not as products to duplicate. The new public documentation hub can link outward to source material. Reciprocal links, homepage modules, app edits, directory registrations, and production-repository changes require separate source-owner authorization and are not included in this publication.

A destination may be reachable yet thin, unready, stale, or commercially unapproved. Record **reachability**, **content status**, **functional validation**, and **commercial availability** as separate facts. The initial observations below are from the research snapshot, not a promise of current application functionality.

## Placement record template

For an actual approved placement, record referral ID; source canonical URL; exact destination; approved copy; placement owner; evidence/review date; proposed versus available state; approved locale; required qualification; approval date; scheduled review; and removal/rollback owner. This is a manual documentation record, not an automation or link-installation instruction.

## Link checking and release rules

1. Check the exact intended HTTPS URL close to publication, including final status and redirect destination.
2. Treat a final HTTP 200 as a reachability result, not functionality, legal compliance, or service readiness.
3. Review unexpected redirects, login walls, soft-error pages, and Coming soon content even if the response is 200.
4. Do not silently substitute an unrelated page to make a link checker pass.
5. If a destination fails or is unready, hold the placement and tell the owner. Do not modify Zion's production repository to repair it under this scope.
6. The requested completion check covers the 12 GitHub document pages and Zion's homepage only. It is not an assertion that every source link in the documentation was reverified at publication.

## Attribution and privacy

Use existing approved analytics or manual reporting. Agree source/campaign labels with the analytics owner before adding tracking parameters. Never place personal information, contract terms, sensitive customer identifiers, or access tokens in URLs. No tracking scripts, pixels, forms, or analytics integrations are included.
