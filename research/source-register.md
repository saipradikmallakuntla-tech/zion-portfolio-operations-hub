# Source Register

**Status: public-source research supporting proposed advisory programs; not an official service catalog or certification.**

Research snapshot: **15 November 2026**. Initial publication edition: **15 November 2026**.

## Method and evidence labels

The planning research used Exa live page-content retrieval and read-only GitHub tools. Firecrawl was not used. The review read public documentation, directory listings, and catalog data; it did not modify Zion's repository, submit forms, authenticate to client systems, exercise app features, or perform a technical/security test.

- **Page observed:** page text was extracted from a live retrieval at the research snapshot. This establishes what the page said, not whether the advertised functionality worked.
- **Listed:** a capability appears in a catalog or document but has not been independently validated.
- **Coming soon:** the inspected destination explicitly indicated preparation or unavailability.
- **Not found:** live retrieval reported HTTP 404 at that time. Do not repair or substitute the destination silently.
- **Historical record:** dated material useful for context, not an authoritative current inventory.
- **External guidance:** primary-source material supporting a risk or opportunity, not evidence of Zion's delivery capability or legal advice.

Exa retrieval success was not treated as an independent HTTP 200 certificate. The requested post-publication checks of this repository's 12 document URLs and Zion's homepage are separate point-in-time HTTPS checks. Other outbound links in these documents are not covered by that 13-URL verification claim.

## Evidence maintenance record

For each material claim, maintain: source ID and exact URL; claim or short excerpt; date reviewed; observed/listed/historical status; source version or blob SHA when available; confidence and limitation; content owner; next review; and the approval decision. Re-review live commercial destinations before campaign use, not merely on a monthly schedule.

Do not copy client contracts or personal information into this register. Keep supplier attestations and client-specific evidence in approved private systems and link only to non-sensitive internal identifiers where an authorized private workflow permits it.
